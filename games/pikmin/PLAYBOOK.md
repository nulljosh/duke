# Pikmin 2 (GameCube PAL) playbook for the bot

Source: pikminwiki.com (Pikipedia), read on 2026-10-29. Where pages disagree, the note says so. Check those points in-game before trusting them.

## 1. Route from Day 4

Pikmin 2 has no day limit. The 30-day limit belonged to the original game. The debt is 10,000 Poko. Story order: Valley of Repose, then Awakening Wood (opened by the Spherical Atlas), then Perplexing Pool (opened by the Geographic Projection from Awakening Wood), then the debt is repaid, then Wistful Wild after the President takes Louie's place.

Suggested order from now:
1. Day 4: finish Emergence Cave. Carry the Spherical Atlas out and leave by the geyser. Returning it to the Pod opens Awakening Wood. Sunset ends the day.
2. Awakening Wood, cheapest first:
   - Hole of Beasts (5 sublevels, Stone of Glory on floor 1, Prototype Detector from the Empress Bulblax on floor 5).
   - White Flower Garden (5 sublevels, 7 treasures). Get the White Pikmin here: Ivory Candypop Buds on sublevel 3 make them.
   - Bulblax Kingdom (7 sublevels, 10 treasures). The Emperor Bulblax drops Forged Courage, which gives the Scorch Guard.
   - Snagret Hole (7 sublevels, 15 treasures). Needs Blue for the water, White for the poison, Yellow for the wires.
   - Blue Onion: behind an electric gate (48,000 HP) on the north side of a big pool near White Flower Garden. Yellow is the color for electric hazards.
3. Yellow Onion (Perplexing Pool): behind a poison gate guarded by a Fiery Bulblax. It needs White and Red, so do White Flower Garden first.
4. Perplexing Pool: Citadel of Spiders (5 sublevels, 11 treasures), Glutton's Kitchen (6 sublevels, 14 treasures, electric gate), Shower Room (7 sublevels), Submerged Castle (5 sublevels). The Submerged Castle Waterwraith gives the Pluckaphone.
5. Valley of Repose: Frontier Cavern (8 sublevels, 15 treasures, mostly buried, White needed). The Rush Boots come from the Repugnant Appendage on the last floor, guarded by an Empress Bulblax. This came from a search snippet only, so verify it.
6. Pay the 10,000 Poko debt with collected treasure value. The wiki does not give an exact trigger.
7. Wistful Wild: rescue Louie from the Dream Den (Titan Dweevil) and finish the remaining treasure.

Must-gets early: Spherical Atlas, Blue Onion, White Pikmin (Ivory buds), Scorch Guard (Emperor Bulblax), Yellow Onion, Pluckaphone.

## 2. Per area

- Valley of Repose: start area. Red Onion is the ship's. Frontier Cavern is White-heavy. Pluckaphone and Rush Boots are optional upgrades that need later caves.
- Awakening Wood: Blue Onion behind an electric gate. 7 overground treasures across 4 caves, worth 765 Poko (US). Gates: white bramble 48,000 HP (x2), poison white bramble 30,000 and 42,000, black bramble 129,000, electric 48,000 (x2). Hazards: fire, poison, electricity, water. Colors: Red for fire geysers, Purple for most enemies, Yellow for electric, White for poison, Blue for water.
- Perplexing Pool: Yellow Onion behind the poison gate. Lakes and submerged caves mean Blue is needed. Beady Long Legs and Spotty Bulbear appear from day 31, per the wiki. Hazards: electricity in Glutton's Kitchen, fire geysers in Citadel of Spiders. Colors: Blue for lakes, Yellow for electric, White for poison, Purple for most fights.

Sources: https://www.pikminwiki.com/Awakening_Wood , https://pikminwiki.com/Perplexing_Pool , https://pikminwiki.com/Yellow_Onion

## 3. Early caves

- Hole of Beasts (Awakening Wood, 5 sublevels): Sheargrubs (only the male needs killing), Mitites, Red Bulborb (floor 4), Empress Bulblax boss (floor 5). Floor 3 has 14 fire geysers: send only Red Pikmin (15 or more). Floor 4: Purples on the Red Bulborb and Mitites. Boss: throw onto the head, whistle them back before they get shaken off, never approach from the side.
- White Flower Garden (Awakening Wood, 5 sublevels, 7 treasures): Bag on the entrance needs at least 12 Purples (the story page says more than 20, so bring 20). Kill the Cloaking Burrow-nit near the entrance first. Floor 3 Ivory buds take 5 Pikmin each and turn out White. Fire and poison hazards. Boss: Burrowing Snagret (see section 5), Five-man Napsack inside.
- Bulblax Kingdom (Awakening Wood, 7 sublevels, 10 treasures): Fire, electricity and bomb-rocks. Floor 4 needs Yellow to cut the wires. Red for the fire geysers. Purples for the Bulborbs, Blowhogs and Beetles. Floor 5: send a leader in alone to set off the bomb-rock. Boss: Emperor Bulblax on floor 7. Destroy the geysers first, then throw Purples at the eyes.
- Snagret Hole (Awakening Wood, 7 sublevels, 15 treasures): Water on floor 6 (only Blue carries through the puddles), electricity on floor 4 (Yellow), five poison emitters on floor 6 (White shuts them). Boss: Pileated Snagret on floor 7 (Justice Alloy). Use Ultra-Spicy spray and keep Pikmin landing on its head.
- Citadel of Spiders (Perplexing Pool, 5 sublevels, 11 treasures): Floor 1 Purples on Mitites and Shearwigs. Floor 2 Reds for geysers, Purples on Wollyhops and Dweevils. Floor 3 Yellows for Anode Beetle electricity. Floor 4 Hermit Crawmads and Water Dumples: Purples, Reds for geysers, Whites for buried treasure. Floor 5 boss Beady Long Legs: dismiss the squad, run in to trigger it, then call them back (see section 5).
- Glutton's Kitchen (Perplexing Pool, 6 sublevels, 14 treasures): Electric gate, so Yellow is required. Kill the Yellow Wollyhop near the gate first. Wiki suggests about 40 Yellow, 30 Purple, 10 Red and 20 White. Floor 6 boss: Giant Breadbug.

## 4. Mechanics the bot gets wrong

- Carry and strength: each Purple counts as 10 Pikmin for carrying and attack strength. The page does not list strength values for other colors.
- Candypop Buds: a standard bud takes 5 Pikmin and then spits seeds. Violet and Ivory buds only spawn if you have fewer than 20 of that type. Buds wither after five transitions. The wiki says buds closing on touch is a Pikmin 3 and 4 behavior, so check bud.py against the real game before changing anything.
- Day length: one day is about 13 minutes of real time, with the in-game clock running 7 AM to 7 PM. Sunset gives a 1 minute 5 second warning, then a 32 second countdown from 10, then the ship lifts off.
- Pikmin left at sunset: safe only if they are in a leader's group, near an activated Onion, near an undiscovered Onion, or buried. Everything else is eaten overnight. More Pikmin left behind means more enemies in the sunset cutscene. A corpse not returned to an Onion by day's end disappears.
- Caves: the wiki does not say whether the clock runs inside a cave. Treat "no time limit in caves" as unconfirmed. Pikmin cannot be grown inside caves.
- Geysers: every cave has one on its lowest floor, and some have earlier ones for leaving when you are short on Pikmin. Leaders and Pikmin jump in and get blown out through the roof. Bulbmin cannot use them. The wiki does not say what happens to Pikmin left on the floor.
- Growing Pikmin: Pellet Posies have 50 HP. Pellet sizes are 1, 5, 10 and 20, and the only 20-pellet posy is in Awakening Wood. Pellet colors cycle red, yellow and blue. The wiki gives no seed count per color, so this needs in-game checks.
- Sprays: Ultra-Bitter spray (10 purple berries) petrifies enemies for about 9 seconds, and most take 1.5 times damage while stoned. Ultra-Spicy spray is more common and sits on the required path in Awakening Wood, but its effect is not on that page.

## 5. Enemy cheat sheet (days 1 to 10)

- Snow Bulborb (Emergence Cave): throw one Pikmin onto its back to kill it. Purples stun it.
- Red Bulborb (Hole of Beasts floor 4): attack from behind. It is stunned by Purples. With 30 or more Pikmin, keep throwing onto its back. With a small squad, lure it, then strike when it falls asleep.
- Dwarf Bulborb: one Pikmin onto its back kills it. Swarm or throw in small groups. Purples in a crowd stun most of the group.
- Water Dumple: swarm it. Purples work best on land. In water, Blues squash it, or pull it out and hit it with Purples.
- Fiery Blowhog: throw a group onto its back. Watch for the inhale, then step out of the line of the stream. Reds are the fire-safe choice. Purples do heavy damage. Keep Blue and Yellow away from the flames.
- Wollywog: use small squads and Purples. Whistle back any Pikmin still in the air, and stay out of its landing shadow.
- Burrowing Snagret (White Flower Garden boss): throw at the head as it emerges, not at the scaly body. Throw many at once so it dies before swallowing. If it gets stuck, it is easy. Call back and throw again if it shakes you off.
- Empress Bulblax (Hole of Beasts boss): throw onto the head and whistle. Avoid its side roll.
- Emperor Bulblax (Bulblax Kingdom boss): destroy the geysers first, then Purples at the eyes.
- Beady Long Legs (Citadel of Spiders floor 5): dismiss the squad, run forward to trigger it, then whistle them back. Ultra-Spicy spray helps.
- Sheargrubs: only the male is a threat. Females are harmless.

## Known conflicts to check in-game

- White Flower Garden entrance: 12 Purples (cave page) vs more than 20 (story page).
- Blue Pikmin: Emergence Cave page says they must be found before leaving the cave, while the Awakening Wood page says they are first found there.
- Candypop Bud closing: listed as a Pikmin 3 and 4 behavior only.
- Rush Boots source came from a search snippet, not a fetched page.
