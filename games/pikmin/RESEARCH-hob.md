# RESEARCH-hob: Hole of Beasts and Awakening Wood (Pikmin 2, GameCube PAL)

Researched 2026-11-03. Sources are pikminwiki.com pages (no-www URLs; the www index.php pages returned 403). Fetched pages were read through a summarizer, so numbers are "as the page states" and worth one in-game check. UNCONFIRMED means not on a fetched page. INFERRED means a reasonable reading of page facts, not stated outright.

Roster assumed: 10 Purple + 10 Red = 20 usable (9 Red plus 1 more). 5 Blues penned.

## Carry math (read first)

- Purple Pikmin carry 10 each, Red carry 1 (https://pikminwiki.com/Purple_Pikmin). Search snippet, not fetched: Doomsday Apparatus weighs 1000 and needs 100 Purples (https://pikminwiki.com/Doomsday_Apparatus).
- INFERRED rule: a treasure moves when the total carry strength on it reaches its weight. "Maximum carriers" is the most Pikmin that can latch on. No fetched page states this outright. The Treasure page only states a Purple-counts-as-1 rule for the post-boss weight drop (https://pikminwiki.com/Treasure).
- Whole squad: 10 x 10 + 10 x 1 = 110 strength. Reds only: 10. Purples only: 100 (INFERRED).
- Fire: Red Pikmin are fire-immune (https://pikminwiki.com/Red_Pikmin). The Fire Geyser page does not say which other types die (https://pikminwiki.com/Fire_Geyser). Treat non-Reds as killable by fire: UNCONFIRMED per type.

## Q1. Hole of Beasts, floor by floor

Layout source: https://pikminwiki.com/Hole_of_Beasts unless noted.

Floor 1
- Enemies: 10 Sheargrubs in the room after the landing site: 6 female (harmless), 4 male (they attack). Only males need killing.
- Treasure: Stone of Glory. Weight 5, value 100 Poko, max 10 carriers (https://pikminwiki.com/Stone_of_Glory).
- Hazards and buds: none listed. Exit to floor 2: not described. UNCONFIRMED.

Floor 2
- Enemies: Mitite group of 10, plus 0 to 2 more groups hatching from 2 nectar eggs in dead ends.
- Treasure: none. Hazards: none listed.
- Buds: 2 Violet. The Violet page says they appear only with "less than 20 Purples" (https://pikminwiki.com/Violet_Candypop_Bud). The check runs once, at the start of the sublevel (https://pikminwiki.com/Candypop_Bud). At 10 Purples they should spawn.
- Exit to floor 3: not described. UNCONFIRMED.

Floor 3
- Enemies: none listed.
- Treasures: Cosmic Archive (weight 15, value 230, max 25) and Strife Monolith (weight 12, value 150, max 20) (https://pikminwiki.com/Cosmic_Archive, https://pikminwiki.com/Strife_Monolith).
- Hazards: 14 fire geysers (7 at "hard" spots, 7 at "special" spots). The Cosmic Archive page says to take out the geysers before retrieving it. Buds: none.
- Exit: the hole to floor 4, near the Cosmic Archive.

Floor 4
- Enemies: 1 Red Bulborb, 10 Mitites from the ground, and 0 to 2 Mitite groups from 2 eggs.
- Treasures: Dream Architect (weight 20, value 280, max 30), held by the Red Bulborb (https://pikminwiki.com/Dream_Architect). Luck Wafer (weight 1, value 140, max 3), in a nook beside a Violet bud (https://pikminwiki.com/Luck_Wafer).
- Hazards: 4 fire geysers, all at "hard" spots. Buds: 1 Violet, always present (no count condition).
- Exit to floor 5: not described. UNCONFIRMED.

Floor 5
- Enemies: Empress Bulblax boss, 3300 HP on this floor, starts asleep (https://pikminwiki.com/Empress_Bulblax).
- Treasure: Prototype Detector (weight 35, value 200, max 45), held by the Empress. If she is beaten with too few Pikmin, the weight drops to match the army, minimum 1 (https://pikminwiki.com/Prototype_Detector).
- Hazards: none beyond her rolling. Buds: none.
- Exit: escape geyser ("leave the cave via the geyser"). Location and type: UNCONFIRMED.

Treasures needing more than 20 carriers by the infobox max: Cosmic Archive (25), Dream Architect (30), Prototype Detector (45, unless the post-boss drop applies). Strife Monolith needs exactly 20. Under the INFERRED weight rule, Purples can move all of them. Reds alone give 10 strength, so Cosmic Archive (15) and Dream Architect (20) need Purples or cleared geysers.

Violet bud math: each Violet bud produces 5 Purples (https://pikminwiki.com/Violet_Candypop_Bud). A bud converts up to 5 thrown Pikmin (https://pikminwiki.com/Candypop_Bud). Thrown Purples come back as leaves. Thrown Reds become Purple seeds and are not returned (INFERRED). So throw only Purples into Violet buds.

## Q2. Dangerous enemies and safe handling

Male Sheargrub (floor 1, 4 of them)
- 50 HP, no regeneration. Surfaces and follows Pikmin that stay near. Bites Pikmin it reaches; caught Pikmin take a short time to chew free. Tears down unfolded stick bridges (https://pikminwiki.com/Male_Sheargrub).
- Purple landing damage is 50 (Purple data, same page). INFERRED: one Purple landing kills a male. UNCONFIRMED in game.
- Safe pattern: keep Reds and Purples out of its bite range. Throw one Purple from outside it. Range in units: UNCONFIRMED.

Female Sheargrub (floor 1, 6 of them)
- Attack damage 0. Harmless to Pikmin and leaders. They only distract, and give 2 seeds (https://pikminwiki.com/Female_Sheargrub). Leave them alone.

Mitites (floors 2 and 4)
- Cannot harm Pikmin directly (attack 0). Their effect is to "cause panic": every non-Purple Pikmin nearby panics and can wander into danger. Purples are immune (https://pikminwiki.com/Mitite).
- Use Purples only. For an egg, throw 2 to 3 Purples onto it in quick succession. The first breaks it, the rest handle what comes out. Purple landings kill Mitites around them. Side hits do no damage. Flowered Purples are preferred for eggs (same page). Each egg has a 5% chance of a 10-Mitite group.
- Keep the Reds back during the fight. They panic and wander (INFERRED).

Red Bulborb (floor 4, holds Dream Architect)
- 750 HP, no regeneration. Eats Pikmin it catches (https://pikminwiki.com/Red_Bulborb).
- Back attack: sneak up from behind and hit it with a stream of Pikmin. The page recommends at least 30 Pikmin before swarming. You have 20. A few Purples do serious damage; 30% stun chance, stun time 5 (units not stated).
- If it wakes early, back off until it turns, then make a new pass. Attacking as it goes to sleep gives a few seconds (same page).
- Suggested policy: skip the Dream Architect unless you reach 30. The floor 4 Violet bud and Luck Wafer sit nearby. Whether they can be reached without passing the Bulborb: UNCONFIRMED.

Empress Bulblax (floor 5)
- 3300 HP on this floor, regeneration 1 per second. Asleep at the start and "completely oblivious", so a sneak attack works (https://pikminwiki.com/Empress_Bulblax).
- Crush: she rolls side to side and crushes Pikmin under her weight (damage radius 50, move speed 125 in the tech table). No stop timer given.
- Shake: when swarmed she raises her body and swings it to knock attackers off. Then she lies down, rolls into one wall and then the other, and returns to the middle after once or twice.
- Page advice: swarm before she fully wakes, throw Purples at her face, and call Pikmin back before she shakes them off. Conflict: the strategy says Purples stun her, but the tech table lists a 0% Purple stun chance here.
- Full loss-free pattern: UNCONFIRMED. The pages give no standing position, throw timing or whistle timing for this fight. INFERRED plan: throw small Purple groups at the face while she sleeps, stay outside the roll path, and whistle back as she starts to rise.
- Larvae: Bulborb larvae come only from "certain" Empress Bulblaxes, and the page lists Frontier Cavern and Hole of Heroes (https://pikminwiki.com/Bulborb_Larva). Hole of Beasts is not mentioned. UNCONFIRMED whether floor 5 spawns larvae. Larvae eat Pikmin.

## Q3. Cloaking Burrow-nit (Pikmin 2)

Source: https://pikminwiki.com/Cloaking_Burrow-nit. HP 300. The page covers Pikmin 2 generally and does not separate the GameCube version.
- Attack: shoots its proboscis a good distance, grabs the first Pikmin it touches, and eats it after a few seconds. One Pikmin per attack. Shakes off Pikmin clinging to it when attacked.
- Cloaking: rests face-down with its shell showing. Rises when something approaches. If Pikmin escape its pursuit range, it returns to rest and re-burrows. Retracts after a chase.
- Weak point: its face. Pikmin that land on the shell slide off.
- Size of the kill: the page recommends a swarm of about 25 Pikmin. Purples have a 30% stun chance.
- Low-loss options as the page describes them:
  - Both leaders punching the face while staying out of the proboscis. The page calls this the option for players worried about losses, but not fully loss-free.
  - Purples thrown at the face or back to stun it. The page does not call a rear throw loss-free.
  - Waiting for it to retract is not a kill method.
- Bot advice: leader punches from the front if the leader can stay out of proboscis reach. Otherwise stay out of its pursuit range. Whether either is loss-free: UNCONFIRMED.
- Location: "one beyond the Spiderworts" on the central route (https://pikminwiki.com/Awakening_Wood). If the bot takes the bridge route, it may never meet it.

## Q4. Awakening Wood: landing site to Hole of Beasts

Sources: https://pikminwiki.com/Awakening_Wood and https://pikminwiki.com/Creeping_Chrysanthemum.
- Landing site: "southwestern portion of the map". Hole of Beasts: "on the northernmost side of this corridor portion", in the western region, which runs north to south. No distance or compass bearing from the landing site is given.
- Route A (direct): a "small bridge that must be constructed" leads straight into the western part. The page does not say where it starts. Then one Creeping Chrysanthemum sits "before the Hole of Beasts".
- Route B (central): two Burgeoning Spiderworts guard a black bramble gate. A Creeping Chrysanthemum guards the western opening. A Cloaking Burrow-nit sits beyond the Spiderworts. Ravenous Whiskerpillars (2, from day 4), a Honeywisp and Spiderwort mold (from day 7) are also in this area.
- Conflict: the Creeping Chrysanthemum page puts one plant "north of the landing site, past the white bramble gate, on the path to the Hole of Beasts". The Awakening Wood page names a black gate. Which gate or gates block the northern path: UNCONFIRMED. Check in game before routing. The black gate's 129,000 HP figure on that page looks wrong: UNCONFIRMED.
- Near the landing site: a Red Bulborb to the west, two Dwarf Red Bulborbs and one Red Bulborb to the north, and an Iridescent Glint Beetle by a puddle (Awakening Wood page). The beetle is UNCONFIRMED as a threat.
- To reach the cave, clear: the Creeping Chrysanthemum on the path, plus either the bridge (Route A) or the black gate and Spiderworts (Route B). Bridge and gate costs are not on the fetched page: UNCONFIRMED.

## Q5. Other things that kill Pikmin

- Water (all non-Blue Pikmin): "Any inapt Pikmin type that touches water will begin flailing at the surface." Drowning needs water deep enough to cover a Pikmin from feet to waist. Blue and Bulbmin are immune (https://pikminwiki.com/Water). Purple is not named. INFERRED: Purples drown too. Keep all non-Blues out of water.
- Yellow Wollywogs (Yellow Wollyhops): 800 HP. They leap and land on Pikmin. The page says a Wollywog killed mid-air does not kill Pikmin below it, implying a normal fall does (https://pikminwiki.com/Yellow_Wollywog). They are "almost always found in the water". Two guard the pool before the Snagret Hole, and a pond lies south of the cave area (Awakening Wood page). Avoid their water.
- Creeping Chrysanthemum: swings its mouth down to catch Pikmin and eats them quickly. After a miss it falls on its back and is briefly vulnerable (https://pikminwiki.com/Creeping_Chrysanthemum). Keep Pikmin out of reach. Swarm it while it is down.
- Male and female Sheargrubs both tear down unfolded stick bridges (https://pikminwiki.com/Male_Sheargrub, https://pikminwiki.com/Female_Sheargrub). Guard the bridge if you build it.
- Fire geysers (floor 3: 14, floor 4: 4): Reds are immune. Pikmin can destroy a geyser, but only fire-resistant types can attack one while it is burning (https://pikminwiki.com/Fire_Geyser). The Purple page says a Purple landing can deactivate a geyser (https://pikminwiki.com/Purple_Pikmin). UNCONFIRMED in game.

## Five-line summary

1. Carry math (INFERRED): with the whole squad, Purples move every Hole of Beasts treasure. Reds alone give 10 strength, so Cosmic Archive (15) and Dream Architect (20) need Purples, and Purples must stay out of live geysers.
2. Throw only Purples into Violet buds (they come back as leaves; thrown Reds are converted, INFERRED). Floor 2 buds spawn only if you have under 20 Purples at sublevel start. Floor 4 always has one.
3. Floor 1: only the 4 male Sheargrubs attack. Mitites never hurt Pikmin but cause panic, so send only Purples to them. Skip the Red Bulborb (750 HP, page wants 30 Pikmin) unless you reach 30.
4. Empress (3300 HP, asleep, rolls and shakes) is the riskiest fight. No loss-free pattern is on the pages. Stay out of the roll path and call back before the shake. Prototype Detector weight drops if she dies with too few Pikmin.
5. Awakening Wood: landing site is southwest; the Hole of Beasts is at the north end of the western corridor, reached by the bridge or past the Spiderworts and black gate. A Creeping Chrysanthemum sits on the northern path. The white-vs-black gate conflict is UNCONFIRMED. Keep Pikmin out of the Wollywog pond.
