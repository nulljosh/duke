# Pikmin 2 (PAL GameCube): Day 6 research, no Red, Yellow or White

Research only, 2026-11-02. WebFetch summaries of pikminwiki pages, so figures are "as the page states them". Anything not seen on a fetched page is UNCONFIRMED. PLAYBOOK.md, RESEARCH-awakening.md and RESEARCH-route.md were read first. Where this file repeats them, it is noted.

Blocked: pikmin.fandom.com Pilgrim_Bulb (HTTP 402), strategywiki and GameFAQs (403, earlier pass).

## 1. Red Pikmin and the Red Onion

- Red Onion location: Valley of Repose. "Louie finds this Onion in the Valley of Repose when he falls out of the Hocotate ship's lower cockpit." https://www.pikminwiki.com/Red_Onion
- A gallery caption on the Red Onion page shows it at the Awakening Wood landing site. The text does not explain how it got there. https://www.pikminwiki.com/Red_Onion
- Onions travel with the ship: in the Pikmin 2 Treasure Completion Cinema "the Onions ascend into the sky alongside the Hocotate ship." https://www.pikminwiki.com/Onion . The Onion page also says Onions "are capable of following the ship at daybreak" (a general statement, not Pikmin 2 specific).
- Seeds with 0 Reds: "If all Pikmin of one type are lost, the Onion produces a single seed the following day, in Pikmin and Pikmin 2." https://www.pikminwiki.com/Onion . So a Red seed should appear the morning after the last Red died. Whether a Red Onion already active in Valley of Repose gives seeds when approached again is UNCONFIRMED.
- Red Pellet Posy in Awakening Wood: "In the Awakening Wood, there are three [10-pellet posies], located to the left of the landing site. The yellow one is near one of the Burgeoning Spiderworts ... the other two (red and blue) are at ground level." https://www.pikminwiki.com/Pellet_Posy . Ground level is the easy reach.
- Posy colour: "Some Posies' pellets change color at regular intervals... the color is fixed when the Posy is destroyed." Pikmin 2 cycle is "red, yellow, blue". https://www.pikminwiki.com/Pellet_Posy . So a posy is only a Red source when it is red at the moment it dies. How to check colour before hitting it: UNCONFIRMED.
- Pellet yield (how many Pikmin a 10-pellet gives) for Pikmin 2: UNCONFIRMED (not on fetched pages).
- Candypop Buds: RESEARCH-awakening.md lists Crimson buds in Bulblax Kingdom with colour "not stated". The wiki does not confirm a Red bud. UNCONFIRMED.
- Valley of Repose has one 10-pellet posy that "changes colors and can be found on a ledge." https://www.pikminwiki.com/Pellet_Posy

Answer to Q1: The Red Onion is in Valley of Repose. It should travel with the ship (Onion page, cinema text). The bot should not count on the gallery caption alone. Path to Reds: (a) red seed from the Red Onion the morning after extinction, if it is in play, UNCONFIRMED; (b) red 10-pellet posy left of the Awakening Wood landing site when it is red; (c) Valley of Repose posy, which is far away.

## 2. The five Blue Pikmin

- Blue Pikmin are first found in Awakening Wood: "chasing a few Wolpoles beyond an electric gate guarded by a Cloaking Burrow-nit" at the north of the map. https://www.pikminwiki.com/Blue_Pikmin
- Freeing them: "Olimar and Louie break the gate with Yellow Pikmin, then whistle them to take them under their command." https://www.pikminwiki.com/Blue_Pikmin
- This conflicts with the brief, which says the Blues are near the landing site in a pen. The wiki does not mention a pen or captivity. UNCONFIRMED whether the bot's Blues are the same five. Check the save's map before acting.
- The Blue Onion is "in a large pool on the northern side of the map", and its circular area "near the White Flower Garden behind an electric gate". https://www.pikminwiki.com/Awakening_Wood
- Electric gates in Awakening Wood (per the wiki): one far north, on the raised section next to a Cloaking Burrow-nit, blocking the Blue Onion; one north of the landing site at the wooden path to Bulblax Kingdom. https://www.pikminwiki.com/Electric_Gate
- Electric gate colour: "Pikmin that are not Yellow Pikmin cannot touch them." https://www.pikminwiki.com/Electric_Gate . Yellow is required for this gate type. Whether Blue is also needed: UNCONFIRMED (the Blue Onion page says Yellow from Perplexing Pool is required, with no Blue mention). https://www.pikminwiki.com/Blue_Onion
- Seesaw blocks: the wiki places them in the northeast, behind a poison white bramble gate (42,000 HP), "after using a weighted seesaw block". Seesaw blocks are not the way to free the Blues. https://www.pikminwiki.com/Awakening_Wood . Note the 5-day run's Blue pickup uses a "seesaw block glitch", which is a glitch and not a safe route. https://www.pikminwiki.com/5_day_run (via RESEARCH-awakening.md)

Answer to Q2: The five Blues are behind an electric gate in the north (per the wiki). They need Yellow Pikmin to break it. Yellow comes from Perplexing Pool, which needs Yellow's own route (Section 4). The Blue Onion is in the same zone. No seesaw route is confirmed.

## 3. Purples only on Day 6

Carry weight: "Purple Pikmin weigh ten times as much as a regular Pikmin" and "These Pikmin have the strength of 10 Pikmin." https://www.pikminwiki.com/Purple_Pikmin . Each Purple = 10 weight.

Treasure weights (Awakening Wood, from the treasure list, which has no minimum-carrier column; the "Weight" column is the number used here): https://www.pikminwiki.com/List_of_Pikmin_2_treasures

| Treasure | Value | Weight | Purples needed (10 each) | Purples-only status |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Sunseed Berry | 170 | 5 | 1 | Reachable on weight. Location conflict (RESEARCH-awakening.md). |
| Hypnotic Platter (Vitamalz cap) | 100 | 4 | 1 | Reachable on weight. Blocker: tree root across path, beside a bag. UNCONFIRMED |
| Healing Cask | 60 | 6 | 1 | Reachable on weight (regional name, see awakening file). |
| Decorative Goo | 80 | 10 | 1 | Reachable on weight. Location not found. UNCONFIRMED |
| Pilgrim Bulb | 55 | 10 | 1 | Needs White Pikmin to dig up (Section 4). Blocked. |
| Air Brake | 100 | 15 | 2 | Pond. "Only Blue Pikmin can use the seesaw blocks that are underwater." https://www.pikminwiki.com/Blue_Pikmin . Blocked for purples-only. |
| Chance Totem | 100 | 15 | 2 | Location not found. UNCONFIRMED |
| Geographic Projection | 200 | 101 | 11 (10 purples = 100) | Short by 1 weight. Also a poison bramble gate (30,000 HP) nearby. |

Bag: "This bag requires the weight of 200 Pikmin to be crushed." It is on the northeast route before the White Flower Garden. https://www.pikminwiki.com/Bag , https://www.pikminwiki.com/Awakening_Wood . The White Flower Garden bag needs 200 weight. 10 purples = 100. Not liftable on Day 6. 20 purples = 200 exactly (this matches the "more than 20" line in the story page, and resolves the 12-vs-20 conflict in PLAYBOOK.md in favour of 20). Bypass: the Purple page says the White Flower Garden bag "can be bypassed by going out of bounds." https://www.pikminwiki.com/Purple_Pikmin . UNCONFIRMED as a bot route.

Caves:
- Hole of Beasts: no gate or bag listed on the wiki entrance data (RESEARCH-awakening.md). Floor 3 has 14 fire geysers. The page says: "As long as you have at least 15 Red Pikmin should pose no problems. Non-Red Pikmin should be whistled to safety if they catch fire." And "If any Purple or other non-Red Pikmin catch on fire, just whistle them to save them." https://pikminwiki.com/Hole_of_Beasts . Floor 4: "throw Purple Pikmin at" the Red Bulborb. Floor 5: Empress Bulblax, no colour given. The cave page recommends "Bringing all Purple Pikmin available to you" and filling with Reds. https://pikminwiki.com/Hole_of_Beasts
- Purples-only on Hole of Beasts floor 3: the page does not say how much damage a Purple takes from a geyser, or whether the whistle-out is reliable. Geyser page: no detail on damage. https://www.pikminwiki.com/Geyser . Verdict: doable on the page's wording, but UNCONFIRMED. Expect losses.
- White Flower Garden: needs the bag lowered (200 weight). Blocked on Day 6 unless the out-of-bounds bypass works (UNCONFIRMED).
- Bulblax Kingdom: electric gate, so Yellow needed. Blocked.
- Snagret Hole: white bramble gate (48,000 HP) and Blue and Yellow and White on the floors (RESEARCH-awakening.md, https://pikminwiki.com/SH). Blocked.

Answer to Q3: On Day 6 with 10 Purples, reachable: Hole of Beasts (with a geyser risk on floor 3), and the purple-weight overground items Sunseed Berry, Hypnotic Platter, Healing Cask, Decorative Goo (location unknown). Not reachable: Pilgrim Bulb (White), Air Brake (Blue), Geographic Projection (1 weight short), White Flower Garden (bag needs 20 purples), Bulblax Kingdom and Snagret Hole (gates).

## 4. Pilgrim Bulb (flower pot)

- Location: "The Pilgrim Bulb is found in the Awakening Wood, and is located on top of a flower pot, completely buried underground." https://pikminwiki.com/Pilgrim_Bulb
- Digging: "White Pikmin are required to dig it up." https://pikminwiki.com/Pilgrim_Bulb . The count is not given: UNCONFIRMED.
- Ramp: the Awakening Wood page says "To the south of the landing site is a small area that contains several flower pots and a small natural ramp that leads to the top of the pots." https://www.pikminwiki.com/Awakening_Wood . The Pilgrim Bulb page says the pot is "just west of the landing site, near the Iridescent Flint Beetle." https://pikminwiki.com/Pilgrim_Bulb . The two pages disagree on direction. The Iridescent Flint Beetle is "Before the ramp leading to the Pilgrim Bulb." https://www.pikminwiki.com/Awakening_Wood
- The Awakening Wood page also says "there is no ramp that allows access to the northernmost pot" in the Piklopedia version, which differs from the in-game area. https://www.pikminwiki.com/Awakening_Wood
- Which side of the ramp to climb, and how to get the Pikmin up it: UNCONFIRMED (no fetched page gives step-by-step).
- Whites: first White source is White Flower Garden sublevel 3 Ivory Candypop Buds (RESEARCH-awakening.md). The White Flower Garden needs the bag lowered (20 Purples). So Pilgrim Bulb is gated behind the White Flower Garden bag.

Answer to Q4: Pilgrim Bulb: the pot is south/just west of the landing site (pages disagree). Use White Pikmin only. Ramp and count are UNCONFIRMED. The bot has 0 Whites, so this is last on the list.

## 5. Recommended order, Days 6 to 10 (for 10 Purples, no Red, Yellow or White)

This is inference from the facts above. No source gives a Day 6 to 10 route for this save state.

1. Day 6 morning: check for a Red seed from the Red Onion (Onion page rule). If one appears, carry it to the red posy on the left of the landing site, if it is red. UNCONFIRMED.
2. Day 6: Hole of Beasts with all Purples. Floor 3: whistle any Pikmin that catch fire. Floor 4: Purples on the Red Bulborb. Floor 5: Empress Bulblax from the head. The page's Red recommendation is ignored. Expect losses. https://pikminwiki.com/Hole_of_Beasts
3. Day 6: Purple-weight treasures that need no gate: Sunseed Berry (1 Purple), Hypnotic Platter (1 Purple), Healing Cask (1 Purple). Decorative Goo (1 Purple) once its location is found. Sources in Section 3.
4. Day 7 onward: grow Purples to 20 to lower the White Flower Garden bag. Violet buds in Hole of Beasts (sublevel 4 always present, and sublevel 2 when fewer than 20 Purples) are the Purple source. https://pikminwiki.com/Hole_of_Beasts (via RESEARCH-awakening.md). Whether a bud turns 5 Pikmin into Purples or one Purple: UNCONFIRMED.
5. White Flower Garden: once the bag is down (20 Purples), take the Ivory buds on sublevel 3 for Whites. Whites are immune to the poison emitters. https://www.pikminwiki.com/White_Flower_Garden , https://www.pikminwiki.com/Awakening_Wood
6. Pilgrim Bulb: with Whites, dig it up from the flower pot. UNCONFIRMED route.
7. Then Yellow: Perplexing Pool needs Geographic Projection (which is 1 weight short for 10 Purples; needs 11 weight units). Yellow Onion needs White and Red to pass the poison gate and Fiery Bulblax. https://www.pikminwiki.com/Yellow_Onion , https://www.pikminwiki.com/Perplexing_Pool
8. Yellow breaks the electric gate in the north for the five Blues and the Blue Onion. https://www.pikminwiki.com/Blue_Pikmin , https://www.pikminwiki.com/Electric_Gate
9. Days 8 to 10: Bulblax Kingdom (Yellow for the electric gate, Red for geysers), then Snagret Hole (Blue, Yellow, White). Day timing is UNCONFIRMED.
