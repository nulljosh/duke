# Pikmin 2 (PAL GameCube): Awakening Wood research

Research only. Pages were fetched 2026-11-02 with WebFetch. A small model summarised each page, so numbers are "as the page states them". Anything not on a fetched page is UNCONFIRMED.

## Headline facts
- Total treasures: 201. Whole-game Poko: 26,985 (US/Switch), 26,885 (Europe/PAL), 26,181 (Japan). Source: https://www.pikminwiki.com/Treasure
- Awakening Wood overground Poko: 765 (US/Switch), 805 (Europe/PAL), 735 (Japan). Source: https://www.pikminwiki.com/Awakening_Wood
- The per-treasure PAL values below add up to 805, so the PAL column is internally consistent.
- The brief's three gate types all exist here: bramble (white, black), poison (poison white bramble) and electric. Source: https://www.pikminwiki.com/Awakening_Wood
- The brief's Emergence Cave figures (4 treasures, 770 Poko, 13 Pikmin) were not checked. UNCONFIRMED.

### Gates and obstacles (Awakening Wood)
- Bag (1): on the northeast route. Needs 200 Pikmin combined weight to lower. Lowering it opens the northeast, including White Flower Garden's entrance.
- White bramble gate (2, 48,000 HP each): one guards Snagret Hole's entrance. The other is not located.
- Poison white bramble gate (2, 30,000 and 42,000 HP): one by Geographic Projection, one near the seesaw blocks.
- Black bramble gate: the list shows 1 (129,000 HP), but the prose mentions two. UNCONFIRMED count.
- Electric gate (2, 48,000 HP each): one at the Blue Onion area, one at the Bulblax Kingdom entrance.
- Clog (1): puddle north of landing site. The page says it "can be drained" and gives no method. UNCONFIRMED.
- Burgeoning Spiderwort mold: grows over the ultra-spicy spiderworts from day 7.
- Which Pikmin colour clears each gate: NOT stated on the Awakening Wood page. UNCONFIRMED for every gate.
- The cave pages give colour guidance for hazards. Applying it to gates is UNCONFIRMED:
  - Fire: Red. Bulblax Kingdom page: keep "enough Red Pikmin to carry the Gyroid Bust" on hand, since it is "surrounded in fire geysers". https://pikminwiki.com/BK
  - Electricity: Yellow. Snagret Hole page: "deal with any electrical wires with Yellow Pikmin". Bulblax Kingdom page: "a considerable amount of Red and Yellow Pikmin should be present". https://pikminwiki.com/SH and https://pikminwiki.com/BK
  - Water: Blue only. Snagret Hole page summary: only Blue Pikmin can carry objects through water. https://pikminwiki.com/SH
  - Poison: White. Snagret Hole page: "Use White Pikmin to deactivate poisonous gas pipes". White Flower Garden page: White Pikmin are "immune to poison". https://www.pikminwiki.com/White_Flower_Garden
  - The Air Brake pond is water. Whether it needs Blue is UNCONFIRMED. Whether wrong colours lose Pikmin at a gate is UNCONFIRMED too, so treat it as a risk.

## 2. Speedrun order, Days 1 to 5
Source: https://www.pikminwiki.com/5_day_run. This is a glitch-heavy 5-day route, not any% or 100%. The page gives no version or region for the route. It says a 5-day run is "probably impossible" on the New Play Control and Switch versions. Days 6 to 10: UNCONFIRMED (no route source obtained). speedrun.com/pikmin2 returned HTTP 403, so it was not checked.

Route as the page gives it, with the page's day numbers:
- [D1] Valley of Repose: take Louie and grow Red Pikmin. Red picked up.
- [D1] Emergence Cave: convert all Red to Purple. Purple picked up. Re-enter (converting the last Red each time) to raise numbers, and collect the Spherical Atlas on an entry that coincides with a Pikmin extinction.
- [D1] Valley of Repose: raise Pikmin numbers, then collect the Courage Reactor to end the day.
- [D2] Awakening Wood: complete Hole of Beasts and White Flower Garden. Find White Pikmin (White picked up). Collect Geographic Projection. Finish the remaining caves and overground treasures. Blue Pikmin picked up via a seesaw block glitch.
- [D3] Perplexing Pool: Yellow Pikmin picked up. Collect all overground treasures, then all caves. The debt is repaid at the end of day 3.
- [D4] Valley of Repose: overground treasures. Then Subterranean Complex and Frontier Cavern.
- [D5] Wistful Wild: overground treasures, then caves.

Page conflict: the History section says the first complete 5-day run used no Yellow Pikmin. The page does not reconcile this.

Non-glitch colour order (search snippet, not a fetched page, UNCONFIRMED): Purple from Valley of Repose unlocks Awakening Wood (day 2). White found in Awakening Wood unlocks Perplexing Pool. Blue in Awakening Wood needs Yellow's help, so Yellow comes from Perplexing Pool and Blue is taken back in Awakening Wood. Order: Awakening Wood (White), Perplexing Pool (Yellow), Awakening Wood (Blue). The 5-day route gets Blue on day 2 with a glitch instead. Source: https://www.pikminwiki.com/5_day_run (search result).

Candypop Buds, Pellet Posies and onions in this area:
- White Flower Garden, sublevel 3: three Ivory Candypop Buds. Throw 5 Pikmin into each, and each becomes a White Pikmin. They do not appear if total White is 20 or more. Source: https://www.pikminwiki.com/White_Flower_Garden
- Hole of Beasts: two Violet Buds on sublevel 2 (absent if 20 or more Purples), one on sublevel 4 (always there). The page does not state the colour, which is inferred to be Purple from the name. Source: https://pikminwiki.com/Hole_of_Beasts
- Bulblax Kingdom: two Violet Buds on sublevel 4 (transform 10 Pikmin into Purple). Crimson buds on S1 and S6 (colour not stated, "use is not recommended" on S1). Ivory on S1 and S3. Source: https://pikminwiki.com/BK
- Snagret Hole: Ivory on S2 (conditional), Ivory and Violet on S3, Ivory, Lapis Lazuli x3 and Queen on S6, Queen x2 on S7. Colours for Violet and Queen not stated. Source: https://pikminwiki.com/SH
- Pellet Posies: the Awakening Wood page lists 11 vegetation, with more appearing after day 10 (including the game's only 20-pellet posy). Counts and timings UNCONFIRMED. Source: https://www.pikminwiki.com/Awakening_Wood. None are listed on any of the four cave pages.
- Onions: only the Blue Onion is covered. Its unlock is UNCONFIRMED.

Days 6 to 10 timing for Pikmin colours is UNCONFIRMED.

- Sub-bosses: Snagret Hole has Burrowing Snagrets on S3 and S6 (not formally labelled bosses). Hole of Beasts has a Red Bulborb on S4 holding the Dream Architect. Other sublevels have no boss named on the page.
- Difficulty order: Hole of Beasts is the simplest (5 sublevels, fire only). Snagret Hole is "the hardest cave found in the area" per its page. The middle two (White Flower Garden vs Bulblax Kingdom) are UNCONFIRMED.
- Entrance order (a search snippet, not a fetched page): Hole of Beasts second, Bulblax Kingdom third, Snagret Hole last. UNCONFIRMED.
- Cave count: one search snippet names a different set of caves (Citadel of Spiders, Glutton's Kitchen, Shower Room). UNCONFIRMED whether these belong to this area.
- Reachability (from the entrance column above): Hole of Beasts has no blocker listed. White Flower Garden needs the bag lowered (200 Pikmin combined weight). Bulblax Kingdom is behind an electric gate. Snagret Hole is behind a white bramble gate. On the page's data, Hole of Beasts is the only cave with no gate or bag in the way. That makes it the natural first cave (inference from the page).

## 4. Glitches and skips
Source: https://www.pikminwiki.com/5_day_run. Every item below is UNCONFIRMED for PAL GameCube. None is a confirmed-safe skip.
- Day 1 extinction glitch (setup, ends the day without saving). Needs a reload. Not suitable for a bot.
- Cutscene timer storage: kill a Dwarf Red Bulborb at the landing site, then end the day while its soul is airborne. Then "start over from last save."
- Ledge clip in Valley of Repose: push a leader up using a Dwarf Red Bulborb. The page says it "will likely take thousands of attempts."
- Pikmin extinction re-entries into Emergence Cave to raise numbers.
- Seesaw block glitch (for Blue Pikmin on day 2) and carrying charge glitch (fast carry over a gate).

Version warning: on the NPC and Switch versions, Blue Pikmin do not respond to the whistle on day 2. The page does not say which version it was run on, so PAL behaviour is unknown.

Spherical Atlas to Sphere Chart: a search snippet says returning the atlas to the Ship's Pod unlocks the Sphere Chart for travel to Awakening Wood. Not seen on a fetched page. UNCONFIRMED. The 5-day page itself has the atlas collected in Emergence Cave on day 1.
