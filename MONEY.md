# Duke Money

How Duke makes money. The company plan is `REVENUE.md` in the Code root; the ledger is `GTM.md`.

## Price and rail

None. Duke has no App Store record and is never sold. There is no Stripe and no in-app purchase.

## What is actually sold

Nothing. Duke plays Duolingo, Factorio, Game Dev Tycoon and FEZ on Joshua's own Mac. Duolingo's terms forbid automation. A sale would put a product on the store that breaks those terms, and the risk is a takedown and an account ban.

## Who pays and why

Nobody. Duke is a personal tool. Joshua runs it on his own machine for his own games, and it stays that way.

## Cost to serve

It runs on Joshua's Mac, not on a server. The Factorio bridge calls a model with the key on that Mac. No one else is billed.

## Where we are

- 2026-10-25: the Conveyer games were folded into Duke.
- 2026-10-25: the menu bar app runs under launchd from `~/Applications/Duke.app`.
- Revenue: no verified number (the App Store Connect key cannot read sales yet). Duke has no store record, so there is nothing to read.

## Role in the company plan

Never sold, so it does not serve a stage of REVENUE.md. GTM.md lists it that way. It is Joshua's autopilot for his own games, outside the plan.

## Next

None. Duke is never sold, and nothing in the plan changes that.

## Million, billion, trillion

**A million.** Duke stays a personal tool, because a company that sold it would be breaking Duolingo's terms.

**A billion.** Duke would need a licensed route with each game's own permission before anyone could sell it.

**A trillion.** A company that size would build its own games, not automate someone else's.

## Change it

No price exists and there is no App Store record, so no command applies. A record would be a new product that Duolingo's terms do not allow.

Anyone who got Duke while it was free keeps it free.

*No App Store record.*
